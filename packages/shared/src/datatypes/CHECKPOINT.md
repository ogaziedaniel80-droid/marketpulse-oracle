# Data Specification: CHECKPOINT (Planned - Disabled by Default)

- **Code:** `CHECKPOINT`
- **Category:** Transport & Security
- **Status:** Planned (Wave 2) - Disabled by default

> [!CAUTION]
> **Safety and Legal Review Required (README Section 9.2 & 22):**
> Reporting on informal checkpoints, border queue delays, or road inspections can put field reporters at direct risk of security harassment, detention, or retaliation by armed actors. This data type is strictly DISABLED by default until explicit institutional and field safety protocols are certified.

---

## 1. Planned Description

Informal delay or wait time (in minutes) and crossing status at interstate borders and transport control posts.
