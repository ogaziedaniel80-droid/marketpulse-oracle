# Data Specification: LPG (Planned - Disabled by Default)

- **Code:** `LPG`
- **Category:** Energy & Fuel
- **Status:** Planned (Wave 2) - Disabled by default

---

## 1. Description

Liquefied Petroleum Gas (cooking gas) availability and retail refill price per standard kilogram.

## 2. Planned Unit & Values

- Unit: 12.5 kg cylinder refill price or 1 kg retail dispense price in minor fiat units.
- Tolerance: 500 bps (5.00%).
