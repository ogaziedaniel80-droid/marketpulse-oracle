# Data Specification: FX_PARALLEL (Planned - Disabled by Default)

- **Code:** `FX_PARALLEL`
- **Category:** Currency Exchange
- **Status:** Planned (Wave 2) - Disabled by default

> [!WARNING]
> **Safety and Legal Review Required (README Section 9.2 & 22):**
> Parallel market foreign exchange rate collection carries substantial regulatory and personal safety risks for informal bureau de change operators and field reporters. This data type is strictly DISABLED by default until comprehensive legal counsel opinions and physical safety risk assessments are approved.

---

## 1. Planned Description

Street/parallel market cash exchange rate observations (e.g. USD/NGN, EUR/XOF) based on actual cash exchanged by verified bureau de change transactions.
