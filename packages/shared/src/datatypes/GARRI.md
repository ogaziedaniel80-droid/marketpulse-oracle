# Data Specification: GARRI

- **Code:** `GARRI`
- **Category:** Food Staples
- **Status:** Active (Launch Wave 1)
- **Target Countries:** Nigeria, Ghana

---

## 1. Unit & Grade

- **Unit:** Standard measure: 50 kg wholesale bag or standard regional paint rubber / derica measure (standardized to 50 kg equivalent for wholesale tracking).
- **Grade / Variety:** White / cream processed cassava flakes (Garri Ijebu or standard white garri, dry, sieved, sour-fermented standard).
- **Condition:** Crispy dry, free from sand/chaff, moisture content < 12%.

---

## 2. Location Granularity

- **Granularity:** Coarse market-level geohash (6 to 8 characters).
- **Pilot Markets:**
  - Mile 12 Market, Lagos
  - Bodija Market, Ibadan
  - Oyingbo Market, Lagos
  - Wuse Market, Abuja

---

## 3. Currency & Decimals

- **Currency:** Local fiat minor units (`NGN` kobo with 2 decimals; `GHS` pesewas with 2 decimals).
- **Representation:** Fixed-point integer (2 decimals).

---

## 4. Consensus Tolerance Band

- **Default Tolerance:** 800 basis points (8.00%).

---

## 5. Evidence Rule

- **Primary Evidence:** Photo of garri bags or standard calibrated basins at market stall with legible price marker or merchant stall placard.
- **Secondary Evidence:** Handwritten invoice or delivery ticket.
